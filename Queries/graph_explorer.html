Places in romance films

Which places are linked to romance films?

This counts distinct films with a TMDB Romance genre. Filming locations and narrative settings are counted separately. This is a genre association, not a measurement of romantic scenes or audience emotion. Place aliases may still be separate entities.

Heritage across genres

Which heritage sites occur across genres?

Filming links to documented historic forts, palaces, monuments and World Heritage sites are included in this single heritage collection. Named components are grouped under their parent site. One film can have multiple genres; compare the film count too. Heritage identities and filming credits retain their own source evidence.

About the graph & downloads

A graph of evidence, with explicit gaps

Films link to songs, cast members, credited roles, genres, filming places and story settings. Playback singers, composers and lyricists come from song credits. A film's cast is not automatically treated as the cast appearing in each song. A film location is never inherited as a song location.

TMDB person IDs identify cast and directors. Name-only music credits are scoped to a source and film and may contain credited duos or groups. Character records preserve full role strings, including self appearances and compound roles, without guessing separate fictional identities.

Events and landscapes have no validated instances in these source files. The ontology reserves these classes for future evidence. No missing facts are filled by a language model. Heritage sites without film links remain available as reference locations for spatial searches.

Coordinates are representative points, often for a city, region or country. Nearby heritage does not imply filming at that site. Review-only extraction candidates are excluded from the graph. Source disagreements and uncertain matches remain in the review tables.

The corpus includes the union of supplied movie records for the selected years; it is larger than the earlier enriched movie CSV. It is not a verified census of Bollywood. The graph view limits the number of visible nodes to keep a film readable; all accepted relationships remain in the downloadable graph and tables.
