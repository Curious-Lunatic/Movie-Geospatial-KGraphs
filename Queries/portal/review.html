What an approval changes

An approval records your decision. Export creates a reproducible curation file. Rebuild applies approved records to the graph and updates the atlas. Other pending records remain separate. The original scrape files are never edited.

Named-site coordinates usually represent the place, not the camera. Exact shooting coordinates require explicit scene evidence and a coordinate source. Source reliability and geographic precision are separate questions.

Review edits are available only through the local server. Public releases show review summaries without a write endpoint.
